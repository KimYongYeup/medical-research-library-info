Medical Research Library

A personal, single-user desktop workflow for organizing medical research literature, bibliographic metadata, search records, and research notes.

What the application does

The operator uses rclone and local scripts to store and retrieve selected, approved research files in their own Google Drive. The workflow uses Google OAuth with the per-file drive.file scope rather than requesting full Drive access. Transfers are initiated by the operator after review.

This website provides application information only. It is not a login portal, a clinical service, a repository of research data, or a service offered to the public. It does not request Google authorization or accept file uploads.
