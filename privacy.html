Privacy policy

Effective November 26, 2026

This policy describes the Medical Research Library Google Drive integration, a personal workflow operated by its owner using rclone and local scripts.

Data accessed and purpose

The integration requests the Google Drive https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This permits access to files and folders created by or explicitly made available to the application, including file content and metadata needed to list, upload, retrieve, and verify authorized research files. The integration uses these data solely to maintain the operator's personal research library. It does not request full Drive access, Gmail, or contacts.

Storage and protection

Selected research files are stored in the operator's own Google Drive and local working directories. Local configuration may contain OAuth credentials and tokens and must be protected with appropriate operating-system access controls. Transfer and verification logs may contain file paths, hashes, and status information. Google Drive transfers use encrypted HTTPS connections. The integration is not intended to process patient-identifiable information, restricted institutional datasets, or materials without permission for external storage.

Sharing and use limitations

The integration exchanges authorized files and metadata with Google Drive to perform its stated function. It is not designed to sell Google user data, provide advertising, publish research files, transmit Drive data to AI training services, or disclose data to unrelated third parties. Separate research and AI tools are outside this OAuth integration and require their own authorization and data-handling review. The integration's use of Google user data is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion and revocation

Research files and related logs are retained under the operator's control until no longer needed. The operator can delete selected files from Drive and local storage, subject to applicable retention obligations and any retained backups. OAuth access can be revoked from Google Account connections. Revocation stops further authorized access but does not itself delete existing copies of research files or local logs. Local credentials and tokens can also be removed when the integration is retired.

Public information site

This static site does not contain a sign-in form, upload form, analytics script, or application-managed tracking cookies. GitHub hosts the site and may process ordinary web request information under its privacy statement.

Contact

For questions about this personal integration, use the support contact shown on its Google OAuth consent screen or the information repository. Do not post confidential information, credentials, or research data in public GitHub issues.
